2. End-to-End Encrypted Patient Messages

Patient messages and device status flags with timestamps are end-to-end encrypted in this browser with AES-256-GCM before being sent to Firebase. Only someone with the passphrase can decrypt them. Firebase never receives the passphrase or plaintext. Use a strong passphrase of at least 12 characters and keep it private; if lost, records cannot be recovered. Firebase record IDs remain visible.
